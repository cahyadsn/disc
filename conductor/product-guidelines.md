# Product Guidelines: DISC Personality Test

## Voice and Tone
- **Friendly and approachable**: Present instructions, questionnaire prompts, and assessment interpretations in warm, constructive, and clear language.
- **Empathetic and Non-Judgmental**: DISC profile descriptions must emphasize strengths, behavioral tendencies, and development areas neutrally without labeling or criticizing.
- **Clear and Jargon-Free**: Avoid excessive psychological jargon; explain behavioral dimensions (Dominance, Influence, Steadiness, Conscientiousness) in practical, relatable terms.

## Design Principles
1. **Simplicity over features**: Keep core assessment workflows lean, accessible, and free of unnecessary dependencies or heavy asset bundles.
2. **Deterministic & Transparent Results**: Calculate dimensions strictly per standard DISC scoring criteria, ensuring reproducible results.
3. **Zero-Friction Integration**: Allow straightforward embedding into existing applications without demanding complex setups or external API calls.
4. **Inclusive & Multilingual**: Structure UI texts and assessment questions so that additional language translations can be cleanly supported.
